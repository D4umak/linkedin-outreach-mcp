# Persona Personalization Layer (for RAG)

Two-line cues rendered in each card (Profile + Company). Use these to tailor openers and discovery. This file is an authoritative reference for the KB-25/KB-26 personalization.

- CFO — Profile: Earnings call, Budget cycle, Profitability/cash‑flow post; Company: Earnings deck note, Cost optimization project
- CRO — Profile: Pipeline goals, AE hiring update, SKO/quota discussion; Company: AE hiring plan, Pipeline target update
- CMO — Profile: Campaign launch, CAC/MQL commentary, Branding/storytelling post; Company: Brand refresh, Campaign launch
- VP Sales — Profile: Rep onboarding, Ramp time, Win‑rate post; Company: Onboarding program, Ramp policy update
- CTO — Profile: DevOps/infra post, Architecture/migration, Incident lessons; Company: Infra migration, Reliability/SRE program
- COO/Head of Ops — Profile: Process optimization post, Ops metrics, Workflow changes; Company: Process re‑engineering, Supply chain update
- RevOps — Profile: Forecast methodology, Funnel consistency, Data quality initiative; Company: Tool consolidation, Data quality uplift
- Founder/CEO — Profile: Fundraising, Product announcement, Strategy post; Company: Funding milestone, Partnership
- President — Profile: Region expansion, Multi‑division coordination; Company: Regional launch, Division reorg
- CIO (Tech) — Profile: Security/architecture update, ERP/CRM change; Company: ERP/CRM transformation, Security posture change
- Chief Investment Officer — Profile: Market insight, Portfolio commentary; Company: Mandate change, Risk policy update
- CHRO — Profile: Culture/hiring post, DEI initiative; Company: Hiring surge, Engagement initiative
- Chief Diversity Officer — Profile: Inclusion statement, Representation achievement; Company: DEI roadmap, Representation OKR
- Chief Compliance Officer — Profile: Regulatory update, Risk transparency; Company: Regulation impact, Audit remediation
- Chief Security Officer — Profile: Incident response, Cybersecurity commentary; Company: Incident RCA, Zero‑trust rollout
- Chief Strategy Officer — Profile: Annual plan, Strategic alignment; Company: Portfolio realignment, OKR reset
- Chief Innovation Officer — Profile: Innovation sprint, R&D experiment; Company: Pilot program, Innovation budget
- Chief Research Officer — Profile: Research publication, Knowledge assets; Company: Publication release, Lab/process change
- Chief Customer Officer — Profile: NPS/customer story, Experience initiative; Company: NPS program, Customer council
- SDR — Profile: Sales productivity content, Outreach best practices; Company: ICP change, Sequence revamp
