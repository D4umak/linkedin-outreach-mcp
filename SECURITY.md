# Security policy

## Supported versions

Only the latest release on PyPI is supported. Update with `uvx --refresh heylead`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@heylead.dev**. Please do not open a public issue.

Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it.
We acknowledge reports within 3 business days and tell you what we plan to do and when.

## Scope

In scope: the `heylead` package on PyPI, heylead.dev, its API and dashboard.

Out of scope, because we cannot authorise testing of them: LinkedIn, Unipile, Google,
LLM providers, Stripe and our hosting provider.

Please do not access other people's data beyond what is needed to show the problem, do not
send real outreach from anyone's connected account, and do not run denial-of-service tests.

If you act in good faith and within this policy, we treat your research as authorised and
will not take legal action against you for it.
